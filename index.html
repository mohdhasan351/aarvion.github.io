Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 3 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Exam Photo Resizer: Reduce KB. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Local Processing (Privacy First)

The core functionality of this app—resizing and compressing photos—is performed locally on your device.

  • Your images are not uploaded to our servers unless you turn on the optional “Help improve Photo Resizer” setting described in section 3a.
  • We never access your device's gallery; only the images you choose to resize are processed.

3a. Optional: “Help improve Photo Resizer”

After your first resize, the app asks once whether you want to help improve it. Nothing is shared unless you tap “Allow”. You can change this at any time in Settings → Help improve Photo Resizer; turning it off stops all further sharing immediately.

  • What is shared: each photo, signature or document image you resize (the image you picked and the resized result), plus the preset name, app version, Android version and device model. Documents can contain personal details such as ID numbers. Images from the multi-page document scanner are not included.
  • What is removed first: the image is re-encoded on your device before sending, removing location (GPS) and all other hidden photo metadata. No name, account, advertising ID or IP address is stored with it.
  • Why: only to test and improve the app's image processing, including training our own image-processing models.
  • How: sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our own server, stored outside any public website, never sold and never shared with third parties.
  • How long: deleted automatically 30 days after it is received. The programme ends on 31 December 2026.
  • Unreliable connections: sending happens in the background only when you are online and is abandoned after a few failed attempts; it never delays your resize.

3. Data Collection and Usage

Apart from the optional sharing in section 3a, we use third-party services that may collect data to help us improve the app and provide advertisements:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app functionality and updates.
  • AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID to personalize ads.
  • Firebase / Google Analytics: We may collect anonymous technical data (e.g., device model, app crashes, and usage statistics) to improve user experience.
  • Microsoft Clarity: We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how users interact with the app's screens, such as taps, scrolling and navigation patterns, so we can find and fix usability problems. This may include on-screen session recordings of app usage, which can show the images displayed on screen.

4. Feedback and Support Requests

If you use the in-app "Report an Issue" feature, it opens your device's email app with a diagnostic log attached so we can help resolve your problem. Your email address is included automatically as part of sending that message, the same as it would be for any email you send. We use it only to reply to your report — we do not add it to a mailing list, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. This feature is entirely optional; the app's core resizing and compression tools work without ever using it.

5. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Storage / Media Access: To select and save images.
  • Internet Access: To load ads and send crash reports.

6. Data Security

Unless you turn on “Help improve Photo Resizer”, your photos remain on your device and their security depends on your device’s operating system. Images shared through that setting are sent over HTTPS, stored outside any public website, and deleted after 30 days. We store no other personal data on our servers.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
